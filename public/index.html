    Credit Cards

    A plan for paying your cards from checking. Bills due before each card come out first and a buffer stays in checking. Then every minimum gets paid, then statement balances, highest interest rate first (a 0% promo last). Paychecks that arrive before a due date count toward it. Anything left over stays in checking.

    Payment plan

    Soonest due first

    “Still owed” is the statement balance minus payments made since the statement. Interest is an estimate for one month. Carrying any balance also ends the interest-free grace period on new purchases until it’s paid off, so paying a statement in full on time comes before extra payments to a card that’s already past its due date.

    Loans

    Your mortgage and loans: rate, payment, payoff date and the full amortization schedule. Try extra monthly payments or a one-time payment to see how much interest you’d save and how many months come off the loan. Your bank shares details for mortgages and student loans; for car and personal loans, click Edit to add the rate and payment.
